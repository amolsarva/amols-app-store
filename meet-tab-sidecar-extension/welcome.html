Chrome extension

Join the Meet. Let the other tab do the work.

Meet Tab Sidecar opens a controlled companion tab for audio, scripted intros, and presentation handoffs. You stay in Google Meet; the sidecar carries the material.

What it does

Stores a Meet URL, a work-tab URL, a spoken intro, and an audio URL. From the popup or Meet overlay, open the sidecar and present that tab with audio.

What Chrome requires

Chrome and Meet require a user gesture to share a tab and enable audio. The extension guides the handoff, but it does not silently join calls or pipe audio into the microphone.

Best workflow

Join Meet, open Sidecar, choose Present now, select A tab, pick Sidecar, keep Also share tab audio enabled, then play the material.
