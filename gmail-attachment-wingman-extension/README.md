# 📎 Gmail Attachment Wingman

**Pull attachments out of Gmail in bulk, without saving them one click at a time.**

A tiny Chrome extension that walks you through deploying a Google Apps Script attachment extractor
in your own Google account: it opens Apps Script, gives you the code to paste (`code.gs.txt`,
`index.html.txt`) and explains each deployment step. The extractor runs as you, in your account;
nothing passes through a third-party server.

**Install:** open `chrome://extensions`, turn on **Developer mode**, click **Load unpacked**, pick this
folder, then click the 📎 toolbar icon and follow the steps.
