**🌙 New in Amol's App Store: iMessage Sync**

Every night, your whole iMessage history becomes a folder people and AIs can read: Markdown per person and per day, a searchable database, and photos, videos and PDFs sorted and shrunk.

Messages hides your conversations in a database no other app can open and quietly offloads old photos. iMessage Sync copies everything out every night and never forgets, even after you clean up Messages. It heals itself: catches up after sleep, retries with backoff, and a doctor command tells you exactly what to fix.

Free, open, runs on your own Mac. Get it: https://amolsarva.com/scripts · Source: https://github.com/amolsarva/amols-app-store/tree/main/imessage-sync
