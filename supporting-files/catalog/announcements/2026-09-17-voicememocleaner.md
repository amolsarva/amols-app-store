**🎙️ New in Amol's App Store: VOICEMEMOCLEANER**

Exports every Voice Memo — original and compressed copies — with title, date, duration, and GPS metadata for analysis and archival.

Voice Memos pile up and iCloud never makes them easy to actually use. This reads the Voice Memos database directly, drops full-quality and space-saving copies of every recording into one folder, and pulls out whatever GPS/time metadata is embedded so the pile becomes something you can sort, search, and listen back through.

Free, open, runs on your own Mac. Get it: https://amolsarva.com/scripts · Source: https://github.com/amolsarva/amols-app-store/tree/main/voicememocleaner
