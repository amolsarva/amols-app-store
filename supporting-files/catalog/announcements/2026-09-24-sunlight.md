**☀️ New in Amol's App Store: Sunlight**

Native Mac controls for a Tasmota smart bulb: moods, slow color compositions, warm start-up and on-device daylight automation. Your bulb's details stay in a private local file.

Smart-bulb apps want accounts and clouds. Sunlight talks straight to a Tasmota bulb on your Wi-Fi: twelve moods, slow compositions, a warm Edison start-up, and a daylight program that runs on the bulb itself, even with the Mac asleep.

Free, open, runs on your own Mac. Get it: https://amolsarva.com/scripts · Source: https://github.com/amolsarva/amols-app-store/tree/main/sunlight
