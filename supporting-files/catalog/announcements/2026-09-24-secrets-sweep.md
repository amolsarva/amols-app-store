**🔐 New in Amol's App Store: Secrets Sweep**

Audits a folder tree for API keys, tokens and credential files that drifted out of your vault, and can move them in.

Keys end up in .env files, notes and old scripts. This finds them and moves them to one private vault with symlinks so nothing breaks.

Free, open, runs on your own Mac. Get it: https://amolsarva.com/scripts · Source: https://github.com/amolsarva/amols-app-store/tree/main/secrets-sweep
