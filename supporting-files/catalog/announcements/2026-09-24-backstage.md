**🎛️ New in Amol's App Store: Backstage**

A menu-bar command center for everything running in the background on your Mac: LaunchAgents, cron, your own scripts, with run history and one-click repairs.

Backstage lists every background job, explains what it does, spots crash loops and missing scripts, and runs your scripts with live output and history.

Free, open, runs on your own Mac. Get it: https://amolsarva.com/scripts · Source: https://github.com/amolsarva/amols-app-store/tree/main/backstage
