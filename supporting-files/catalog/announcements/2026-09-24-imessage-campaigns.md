_Status: draft. Edit, then post (LinkedIn / X / newsletter). Delete this line when posted._

**📣 New in Amol's App Store: iMessage Campaign Studio**

Find people by what you've talked about before, review each one, then send separate personal texts, with dry-run first and reply-aware follow-ups.

Group texts are rude and BCC doesn't exist in Messages. This searches your own history for everyone who, say, came to last year's party, lets you review the list with context, and sends each person their own message.

Free, open, runs on your own Mac. Get it: https://amolsarva.com/scripts · Source: https://github.com/amolsarva/amols-app-store/tree/main/imessage-campaigns
