**🛟 New in Amol's App Store: Google Photos Space Rescue HQ**

Merge a Google Takeout zip set, sort it into flat PHOTOS and per-year VIDEOS, and repair what Takeout breaks.

Getting a child's photo library out of Google is not one problem but five: the download arrives as fifteen 10GB zips, half the videos are Live Photo clips that count against your quota, thousands of files are duplicates, the extensions lie about what the files actually are, and the true capture dates live in sidecars Google ignores on re-upload. This handles all of it in one place, dry-running every stage and deleting nothing.

Free, open, runs on your own Mac. Get it: https://amolsarva.com/scripts · Source: https://github.com/amolsarva/amols-app-store/tree/main/rescue-hq
